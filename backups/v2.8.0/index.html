🛡️ About WashGuard

WashGuard is a privacy-first web & mobile application for simulating IRS Section 1091 wash sale mechanics. All calculations run strictly client-side on your device—no accounts, no servers, no trade logging.

The 61-Day Window Rule

The wash sale window spans 30 days prior to the sale date, the sale date itself, and 30 days after (61 total calendar days). Buying substantially identical shares during this window disallows the loss for current tax deductions.
