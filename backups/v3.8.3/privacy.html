LEGAL & DATA PRIVACY COMPLIANCE

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2, 2026 • Last Updated: November 2026

1. Client-Side Computation & Zero Server Retention

WashGuard is designed with an uncompromising privacy-first architecture. All trade simulations, lot dates, share quantities, prices, cost bases, and wash sale calculations are performed strictly client-side within your web browser using JavaScript.

🛡️ Zero Data Retention: WashGuard never transmits, uploads, logs, or stores your financial transactions, portfolio balances, broker statements, or tax identification numbers on external servers.

2. Local Storage & Browser Caching

WashGuard utilizes standard HTML5 LocalStorage and Service Worker caching strictly to enhance your local user experience, including:

  • Saving your UI preferences (e.g. active color themes and calculation presets).
  • Storing optional offline Progressive Web App (PWA) cache files so the calculator functions without an active internet connection.

You can clear this local data at any time through your browser's history or cookie settings.

3. Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising

We use Google AdSense and third-party advertising companies to serve advertisements when you visit our website. These companies may use cookies, web beacons, and similar technologies to collect information (not including your name, address, email address, or telephone number) about your visits to this and other websites in order to provide advertisements about goods and services of interest to you.

Mandatory Google Advertising Disclosures:

  • Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on WashGuard.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visit to WashGuard and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting AboutAds.info Choices or Google Ads Settings.

4. Analytics & Telemetry

We may use anonymized web analytics (such as Google Analytics 4) to monitor aggregated traffic patterns, device display categories (desktop vs. mobile), and feature engagement. Analytics cookies do not contain identifiable portfolio data or personal identification.

5. GDPR & CCPA/CPRA Rights

If you are a resident of the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, or California, you are entitled to certain privacy rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). Because WashGuard does not harvest or retain personal identifiers or transaction histories on any central database, we do not sell or share personal consumer data.
